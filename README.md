A one-line excuse from a robot vacuum for missing a spot, written by a small program (`main.ts`) that outputs it to `/output/entry.txt`. The vacuum blames its own lidar and map — it saw the crumbs, panicked, and filed them under "furniture".

> My lidar saw the crumbs, panicked, and filed them under 'furniture' — I never argue with my own map.
